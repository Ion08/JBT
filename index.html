About

We learned how to build robots.
Now we’re learning how to make them fight.

JBT is a combat robotics team from Moldova. The name comes from the initials of the three founders — Johnny, Bogdi, Tony — and the project exists for one purpose: to design, build and fight robots.

RoboRangers → JBT · Since 2017

We didn’t start with combat robotics.
We started with FIRST — and RoboRangers made us who we are.

JBT was created by members of RoboRangers — Moldova’s most decorated educational robotics team, 4th generation, built at Tekwill Academy Kids. The years spent in FIRST LEGO League, FIRST Tech Challenge and FIRST Global Challenge are the foundation this project is built on.

JBT is a new direction: mechanical engineering, electronics, CAD, 3D printing and strategy brought together where the machine has to take a hit and keep moving. Everything we learned on world stages now goes into a combat chassis.

Glaze, but earned: RoboRangers is the team every Moldovan kid knows. Since the National Educational Robotics Program launched in 2014, Tekwill Academy Kids trains ~1,000 kids a year (≈300 in robotics) — RoboRangers is its flagship. 4 generations in, it’s the most consistent medal factory Moldova has: from world champion alliances to the most prestigious juried awards in FIRST.

Coached by Florin Cazac (Robotics Programs, Tekwill) — national medals in 5 years straight, competing head-to-head with powerhouses like Romania within just two FTC seasons and beating 100k+ students from 81 countries. That culture — critical thinking, strong docs, strategy, gracious professionalism — is exactly what JBT brings to combat robotics.
